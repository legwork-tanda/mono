Glass + Tape · generated from tokens/tokens.json

Black, white and one yellow.

Frosted glass over black-and-white photos and the map. Hazard tape marks only the request in flight and the live booking. Everything below is rendered from the same tokens the Swift file is generated from.

The rules

Break one of these and it isn't Legwork.

One accent, always a fill#FFE37A carries every primary action with ink on it (13.2:1). Never yellow text, never yellow on white without ink.
No status coloursNo red, no green. Errors: 2px ring and a bold note. Safety: an ink notice.
Glass, not cardsPanels float over a B&W photo or the map. The only solid cards are on the yellow Request sent screen.
Tape is rareRequest in flight, the booked card, the live job. Labels only, never body text.
One action per screenThe yellow pill moves the job forward. Everything else is glass, a link or a chip.
Press is .96No hover. Words always confirm what motion shows. Reduce Motion skips to the result.
