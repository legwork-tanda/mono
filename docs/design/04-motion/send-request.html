Legwork · motion storyboard v3 · Send request

The page folds into a paper plane.

Tap Send and the Check page itself folds into a paper plane, full size, on yellow. The plane glides home and dissolves into a sent icon on your new job. No extra screen in between.

Paper plane

The page you just checked is the thing that gets sent. It tucks its bottom (with the Send button) under to get paper proportions, then folds four times at full size, so the job details stay printed on the wings. Then home dissolves in, and the plane glides into the new job's slot and becomes its paper-plane icon. No Request sent screen, no Cancel, nothing else to read.

Rules it follows

From the design skills in lovish.EA (better-ui, better-accessibility, service-booking-flow).

The page you checked is the thing that goesNo extra layer and no new metaphor: the Check page itself crunches down and folds up, with your details still printed on it.
Yellow while it foldsYellow shows behind the page as it folds, then home dissolves back over it. Black, white and #FFE37A only.
Paper keeps the page's layoutHeader, title, card and button stay where they were, so the paper is recognisably the page you were just on.
Words carry the meaningThe new row says "Request sent" and "waiting for a yes". The plane is never the only signal. (better-ui)
Press is 96%Send scales to .96 on tap, never below .95. (better-ui · scale on press)
Reduced motion: show the resultWith Reduce Motion on, Send cross-fades straight to home with the sent job on top. (better-accessibility · motion)
Tap to skip, no extra screenTapping jumps straight to home. There's no Request sent page to dismiss, so nothing waits on the animation. (better-accessibility · autoplay)
Under 3 secondsThe plumbers already have the job; the plane covers the first wait instead of adding one.
